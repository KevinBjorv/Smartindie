Most Beginners Don't Need More Tutorials. They Need Direction.

Most beginner game developers do the same thing. They watch tutorials, start random projects, change ideas, get overwhelmed, and never finish anything.

Smart Indie exists to fix that. The goal is not to teach you every tiny feature of a game engine. The goal is to help you start small, build momentum, and finish your first real game.
